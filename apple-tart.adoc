= Apple Tart
:toc:

This recipe makes one 23 cm apple tart. It uses a shortcrust pastry with sliced apples and cinnamon. Allow about 30 minutes to prepare the pastry, 30 minutes for chilling, and 40 minutes for baking.

== Ingredients

You will need:

* 250 g plain flour
* 125 g cold butter
* 50 g caster sugar
* 1 egg
* 1–2 tablespoons cold water
* 5 medium cooking apples
* 40 g caster sugar
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 15 g butter

== Equipment

You will need:

* 23 cm tart tin
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Knife
* Apple peeler or vegetable peeler
* Baking paper
* Wire rack
* Pastry brush

== Before You Begin

Read through the recipe before starting and make sure you have all the ingredients and equipment.

The butter should remain cold while making the pastry. Take it from the refrigerator when you are ready to begin.

Preheat the oven when you are ready to bake the tart.

== Prepare the Pastry

Put the flour and sugar into a mixing bowl. Cut the cold butter into small pieces and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.

Beat the egg and add it to the mixture. Mix until the pastry starts to come together. Add a little cold water if necessary, but do not add too much because this can make the pastry tough.

Wrap the pastry and put it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes. Chilling the pastry makes it easier to roll and can help prevent it shrinking during baking.

If the pastry is difficult to roll after chilling, leave it at room temperature for a few minutes before rolling it.

== Prepare the Apples

Peel and core the apples, then cut them into thin slices. Try to make the slices a similar thickness so that they cook at the same rate.

Put the apples into a bowl and add the sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice. Mix gently until the apples are coated.

Cooking apples give a softer filling and a more acidic flavour. Eating apples can also be used if you prefer a sweeter tart.

== Prepare the Tart Tin

Lightly grease the tart tin with butter.

You can put a circle of baking paper in the base of the tin to make the tart easier to remove after baking.

== Assemble the Tart

Take the pastry out of the refrigerator and roll it out on a lightly floured surface. Roll it into a circle slightly larger than the tart tin.

Carefully place the pastry into the tin and press it gently into the base and sides. Trim away any pastry that hangs over the edge.

Prick the base several times with a fork. Put the prepared apples on top of the pastry and arrange them in an even layer.

Dot the butter over the apples. If you have leftover pastry, you can use it to make small decorations for the top of the tart.

== Bake the Tart

Preheat the oven to 190°C.

Put the tart on the middle shelf of the oven and bake for about 40 minutes. The pastry should be golden brown and the apples should be soft.

If the pastry starts to brown too quickly, cover the tart loosely with aluminium foil for the remaining baking time.

If the pastry is golden but the apples are still firm, continue baking with the tart covered with foil. Different varieties of apple can take different amounts of time to cook.

== Cool and Serve

Remove the tart from the oven and leave it in the tin for about 15 minutes. This gives the filling time to settle and makes the tart easier to cut.

The tart can be served warm or at room temperature with cream, custard or vanilla ice cream.

If you are serving the tart later, transfer it to a wire rack and allow it to cool completely.

== Storing the Tart

The tart can be kept at room temperature on the day it is made. Cover leftovers and refrigerate them if they will not be eaten within a day.

Bring the tart to room temperature before serving if it has been refrigerated.

== Troubleshooting

If the pastry cracks while you are rolling it, leave it for a few minutes to soften slightly before trying again.

If the pastry shrinks during baking, it may have been stretched when it was placed in the tin. Chilling the pastry before baking can help.

If the pastry becomes difficult to handle, return it to the refrigerator for a few minutes.
