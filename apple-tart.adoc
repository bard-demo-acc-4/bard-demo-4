= Apple Tart
:toc:

This recipe makes one 23 cm apple tart. It serves 8–10 people. Total time: ~2 hours (30 minutes prep, 30 minutes chilling, 40 minutes baking).

== Ingredients

* 250 g plain flour
* 125 g cold butter
* 15 g butter
* 50 g caster sugar
* 1 egg
* 1–2 tablespoons cold water
* 5 medium cooking apples
* 40 g caster sugar
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice

== Equipment

* 23 cm tart tin
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Knife
* Apple peeler or vegetable peeler
* Baking paper
* Wire rack
* Pastry brush

== Before You Begin

* Read the entire recipe before you start.
* Gather all ingredients and equipment.
* Keep the butter cold until you use it.
* Preheat the oven to 190°C when you are ready to bake.

== Make and Chill Pastry

1. Combine the flour and sugar in a mixing bowl.
2. Cut the cold butter into small pieces and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
3. Beat the egg and add it to the mixture.
4. Mix until the pastry just begins to come together. Add cold water only if necessary—do not add too much, which makes the pastry tough.
5. Wrap the pastry and chill it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes. Chilling makes the pastry easier to roll and helps prevent shrinking during baking.

[TIP]
====
If the pastry proves difficult to roll after chilling, let it rest at room temperature for a few minutes to soften slightly before rolling.
====

[WARNING]
====
If the pastry cracks while you roll it, let it rest a few minutes to soften, then try again. Do not overwork the dough or add too much water—this makes the pastry tough and hard to handle.
====

== Prepare the Filling and Tin

=== Prepare the Apples

1. Peel and core the apples.
2. Slice the apples thinly and evenly so they cook at the same rate.
3. Put the apple slices into a bowl and add the sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice.
4. Toss gently until the apples are coated.

*Note: Cooking apples give a softer, tangier filling. You can use eating apples for a sweeter tart.*

=== Prepare the Tart Tin

1. Lightly grease the tart tin with butter.
2. Optionally, place a circle of baking paper in the base to make removal easier after baking.

== Assemble and Bake

=== Assemble the Tart

1. Remove the pastry from the refrigerator.
2. Roll it out on a lightly floured surface into a circle slightly larger than the tart tin.
3. Line the tin with the pastry and press it gently into the base and sides.
4. Trim any excess pastry that hangs over the edge.
5. Prick the base several times with a fork.
6. Arrange the prepared apples in an even layer over the pastry.
7. Dot small pieces of butter across the apples. Use leftover pastry to cut small decorations for the top, if you like.

[WARNING]
====
If the pastry shrinks during baking, you may have stretched it when lining the tin. Chill the pastry thoroughly before baking and handle it gently during assembly.
====

=== Bake the Tart

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
2. Place the tart on the middle shelf and bake for about 40 minutes.
3. Check that the pastry is golden brown and the apples are soft.

== Cool, Serve, and Store

=== Cool the Tart

1. Remove the tart from the oven.
2. Let it rest in the tin for about 15 minutes to allow the filling to settle and to make cutting easier.
3. Transfer the tart to a wire rack and let it cool completely, or serve while warm.

=== Serve

Serve the tart warm or at room temperature with:

* Cream
* Custard
* Vanilla ice cream

=== Store Leftovers

1. Same day: Keep the tart uncovered at room temperature.
2. Next 1–2 days: Cover and refrigerate.
3. Before serving: Bring a refrigerated tart to room temperature.
