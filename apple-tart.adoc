= Apple Tart
:toc:

This recipe makes one 23 cm apple tart. It serves 8–10 people. Total time: ~2 hours (30 minutes prep, 30 minutes chilling, 40 minutes baking).

== Ingredients

* 250 g plain flour
* 125 g cold butter
* 50 g caster sugar
* 1 egg
* 1–2 tablespoons cold water
* 5 medium cooking apples
* 40 g caster sugar
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* 15 g butter

== Equipment

* 23 cm tart tin
* Mixing bowl
* Rolling pin
* Knife
* Apple peeler or vegetable peeler
* Baking paper
* Wire rack
* Pastry brush

== Before You Begin

* Read through the entire recipe before beginning.
* Gather all ingredients and equipment.
* Keep the butter cold until you are ready to use it.
* Preheat the oven to 190°C when you are ready to bake.

== Make and Chill the Pastry

1. Put the flour and sugar into a mixing bowl.
2. Cut the cold butter into small pieces and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
3. Beat the egg and add it to the mixture.
4. Mix until the pastry starts to come together. Add cold water only if necessary—do not add too much, as this makes the pastry tough.
5. Wrap the pastry and place it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes. Chilling makes it easier to roll and helps prevent shrinking during baking.

[TIP]
====
If the pastry is difficult to roll after chilling, leave it at room temperature for a few minutes to soften slightly before trying again.
====

[WARNING]
====
If the pastry cracks while you are rolling it, leave it for a few minutes to soften slightly, then try again. Do not overwork it or add too much water—this makes the pastry tough and difficult to handle.
====

== Prepare the Filling and Tin

=== Prepare the Apples

1. Peel and core the apples.
2. Cut them into thin slices. Make the slices uniform in thickness so they cook evenly.
3. Put the apples into a bowl and add the sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice.
4. Mix gently until the apples are coated.

*Note: Cooking apples give a softer filling with more acidic flavor. Eating apples can also be used if you prefer a sweeter tart.*

=== Prepare the Tart Tin

1. Lightly grease the tart tin with butter.
2. Optionally, place a circle of baking paper in the base to make removal easier after baking.

== Assemble and Bake

=== Assemble the Tart

1. Remove the pastry from the refrigerator.
2. Roll it out on a lightly floured surface into a circle slightly larger than the tart tin.
3. Carefully place the pastry into the tin and press it gently into the base and sides.
4. Trim away any pastry that hangs over the edge.
5. Prick the base several times with a fork.
6. Arrange the prepared apples on top of the pastry in an even layer.
7. Dot small pieces of butter over the apples. Use leftover pastry to make small decorations for the top, if desired.

[WARNING]
====
If the pastry shrinks during baking, it may have been stretched when placed in the tin. Ensure you chill the pastry thoroughly before baking and handle it gently during assembly.
====

=== Bake the Tart

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
2. Place the tart on the middle shelf of the oven and bake for about 40 minutes.
3. Check for doneness: the pastry should be golden brown and the apples should be soft.

[NOTE]
====
If the pastry starts to brown too quickly, cover the tart loosely with baking paper for the remaining baking time. Different apple varieties cook at different rates—if the pastry is golden but the apples are still firm, continue baking with the tart covered.
====

== Cool, Serve, and Store

=== Cool the Tart

1. Remove the tart from the oven.
2. Leave it in the tin for about 15 minutes. This allows the filling to settle and makes the tart easier to cut.
3. Transfer the tart to a wire rack and allow it to cool completely, or serve while warm.

=== Serve

Serve the tart warm or at room temperature with:

* Cream
* Custard
* Vanilla ice cream

=== Store Leftovers

* **Same day:** Keep uncovered at room temperature.
* **Next 1–2 days:** Cover and refrigerate.
* **Before serving:** Bring refrigerated tart to room temperature.
